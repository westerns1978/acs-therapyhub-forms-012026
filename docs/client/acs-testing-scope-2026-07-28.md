# ACS TherapyHub — What to test this week

**28 July 2026**

A quick note on where to point your attention, so nobody spends time reporting something that's
simply not switched on yet.

---

## Ready to use

These are the areas worth exercising properly this week:

- **New client intake** — the public intake page through to placing and activating a client
- **Client records** — client workspace, overview, assessment and placement
- **Forms** — assigning forms, filling them in, and the client portal side of that
- **Scheduling** — booking, rescheduling, cancelling, and the day and week calendar views
- **Group sessions** — the Green Room pre-session view and group note distribution
- **Documents** — uploading, categorising, and requesting a document from a client
- **Billing and payments** — charges, the ledger, and recording payments
- **Compliance Readiness** — the deterministic status view
- **The client portal** — dashboard, forms, appointments, billing

---

## Switched off while being finished

A few areas are hidden from the menus at the moment. They're mid-rebuild and would give you
misleading results rather than useful ones, so they're off rather than half-right:

- The older Compliance summary page (**Compliance Readiness** is the current one — use that)
- The in-session transcription screen
- Messages
- Reporting and the document-intelligence tools
- The video-sessions screen
- The client-facing recovery plan wizard

If you go looking for one of these and get bounced back to the dashboard, that's deliberate —
not a bug, and nothing to write up.

---

## Two things to know as you go

**Nothing is sent to clients automatically.** No emails, no texts. When you assign a form or
request a document, you pass it to the client yourself. Automatic messaging is a separate piece
of work and depends on some carrier registration that isn't finished.

**Signatures are typed, not drawn.** Where a form asks for a signature today, it takes a typed
name. Proper handwritten signature capture is the next build, and it's the thing you flagged on
the 21st.

---

## Reporting back

The most useful feedback is where the software made you hesitate, or made you work in an order
that isn't how the practice actually runs. That's harder to spot later and more valuable than a
list of cosmetic issues.

For anything that looks plainly broken, the screen you were on and what you'd just clicked is
usually enough for me to find it.
