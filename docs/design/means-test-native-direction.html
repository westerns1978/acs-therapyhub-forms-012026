Design direction · not a specification

Native Standard Means Test (MO 650-0216)

Bring the Missouri Standard Means Test into ACS TherapyHub as a native staff console, so the fee determination it produces becomes structured data the rest of the system can use — rather than a paper form re-keyed into three other places.

The decision that shapes everything else: this is a staff console, not client intake. David, 21 July 2026 — the form is too complex to hand to a client; staff will sit with them and run it. Every design choice below follows from that: it is optimised for a trained operator moving fast, not for a first-time user being guided.

Why native rather than a PDF

The means test currently ends at a signed sheet of paper. Everything downstream of it — what the client owes, the Notice of Cost, the completion certificate — restates a number that lives only on that sheet. Making the determination native turns it into one authoritative record that the billing ledger, the Notice of Cost, and the certificate all read from.

The gain is not data entry speed. It is that the fee a client is charged becomes traceable to the determination that set it.

The flow

  1. Income capture, in whatever period the client actually gets paid Weekly, fortnightly, twice-monthly, monthly. Staff enter what the client reports; the console converts. Nobody should be doing arithmetic on a legal pad — that is where the errors are today.
  2. Automatic pay-period conversion to a common basis One conversion step, shown on screen, so the operator can see the working rather than trust it.
  3. Household total Income sources combined into the total the rate table is read against.
  4. Rate-table lookup Total plus household size resolves to the fee. The table is configuration, not code — it changes on the state's schedule, not ours.
  5. Certification and e-signature The client certifies the income figures; staff certify they ran the test. This is the point the determination becomes binding, so it is also the point that must carry a real signature — see the dependency below.
  6. Structured determination written Append-only, superseded rather than edited — the same shape as placement_determinations, which already works this way.
  7. Downstream consumers read it Billing ledger, Notice of Cost (MO 650-0215), completion certificate (MO 650-7743).

Dependencies

Signature capture blocking

Step 5 is a certification. Today the system accepts a typed name in a text field, with no signer identity bound to it and no audit stamp. A means-test certification signed that way is weaker than the paper it replaces. The signature build should land first, or the means test inherits the same gap on a document that determines what someone pays.

Rate table as configuration decided

Held as versioned configuration with an effective date, not compiled in. A determination records which version it was computed against, so an old determination stays explicable after the table changes.

Open questions

  • Household composition open — what counts as household size, and who is asked. Drives the lookup.
  • Verification open — is documentary proof of income required, attached, or taken on attestation?
  • Re-testing open — when circumstances change mid-programme, does a new determination supersede, and what happens to charges already raised?
  • Who may certify open — any staff member, or a defined role?
  • Sliding-scale edges open — behaviour at the boundaries of the table, and whether a zero-fee determination is a distinct outcome.

Deliberately out of scope

  • Any client-facing means-test entry. Staff run this — that is the premise.
  • Automatic income verification against external sources.
  • Retrospective recalculation of historical charges.
